# Storm Paycheck — Your First Paycheck (Money at 16 · Day 1)

Interactive 90-minute EL lesson (grades 9–12, ELPA21 Levels 3–4), Monday of the Money at 16 week at Laurel High School. Part of the Storm Studio / EL Publishing resource ecosystem.

**Live page:** https://radan55.github.io/stormpaycheck/  
**Agenda board:** https://radan55.github.io/stormpaycheck/agenda.html

What's inside: bell ringer, $100-bill sketchpad, hook poll, 60-second pay stub puzzle, Money Instructor video with autograded checks, six-paragraph read-aloud passage with Spanish toggles and self-checking stop-and-jots, vocabulary notebook and matching game, numbers-in-sentences grammar move (*percent of* + singular verb / *$X of the $Y was*), a live pay stub builder, five ELPA21-style test prep items with audio (pay stub, word meaning, two details, author's purpose, listening), ELPA21-aligned writing rubric, O·R·E·A·C exit ticket, progress bar, and a full report sent to Jotform.
